6 Best Website Analytics Tools for 2026 (Compared)

We tested the most popular privacy-friendly analytics tools side by side — setup time, dashboard clarity, pricing and features — so you don't have to.

A Aljaž·Oct 8, 2026·9 min read

Picking an analytics tool in 2026 is a different game than it was five years ago. Privacy regulation matured, third-party cookies died for good, and founders got tired of dashboards that need a tutorial. Here are the six tools we'd actually recommend — and who each one is for.

What we compared

Every tool was judged on the same four criteria:

  • Setup time — minutes from signup to first data point.
  • Dashboard clarity — can a non-analyst answer “where do visitors come from?” in under a minute?
  • Privacy — cookies, personal data, consent banner requirements.
  • Pricing — what it really costs at 100k monthly events.
“The best analytics tool is the one your team actually opens. Everything else is shelfware with a chart.”

If you want one dashboard for traffic and product behavior, start with Seline. If you only need pageview counts, Plausible or Fathom will serve you well.
