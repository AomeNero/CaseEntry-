Pricing questions

What happens if I exceed my event limit?+
Nothing breaks. We keep collecting your data and notify you to upgrade. We believe analytics should never go dark on the day your traffic spikes.
Is there a free plan?+
Every account starts with a 30-day free trial with all features unlocked. After the trial, plans start at $9/month — less than the coffee your team drinks while reading GA docs.
Do you offer discounts?+
Yes — pay yearly and get roughly two months free (−17%). Open-source projects and nonprofits can also apply for a discount.
Can I change plans later?+
Anytime, in one click. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply from the next billing cycle.
