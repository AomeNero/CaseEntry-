Frequently asked questions

How is Seline different from Google Analytics?+
Seline shows one clean dashboard instead of hundreds of reports. It's cookieless, collects no personal data, and loads in under a kilobyte. You get the numbers you need in seconds — not a certification course.
Do I still need a cookie banner?+
In most cases, no. Because Seline sets no cookies and stores no personal data, most websites can remove their consent banner entirely. We still recommend a quick check with your legal team for your specific jurisdiction.
Where is my data stored?+
All data is hosted on servers inside the European Union. We never sell or share your data with advertisers — our business is your subscription, nothing else.
What counts as an event?+
A pageview, a custom event, or an outbound click — anything a visitor does on your site. You pay for your total monthly event volume, and every feature is included at every volume.
Can I try it for free?+
Yes — every plan starts with a 30-day free trial, no credit card required. If Seline isn't for you, export your data and walk away. We'll even help you uninstall the script.
